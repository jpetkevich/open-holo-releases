# Open Holo

Open Holo is a free app for collectors of trading cards (Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh! and Disney Lorcana). It keeps your collection on your own device: card data comes straight from public sources, and there's no account and no Open Holo server.

## Download

- **iPhone and iPad:** the App Store (link coming).
- **Mac:** the App Store, later.
- **Windows 10 or 11, and Linux:** coming. Installers will be on the [releases page](https://github.com/jpetkevich/open-holo-releases/releases), each with `SHA256SUMS` so you can check a download (`sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`).
