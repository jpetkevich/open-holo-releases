# Holofoyl

Holofoyl is a free app for collectors of trading cards (Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh! and Disney Lorcana). It keeps your collection on your own device, with no account. Card data, prices and small card pictures come from Fordyce's card data service, built from public sources; full-size pictures come from those sources directly.

## Download

- **iPhone and iPad:** the App Store (link coming).
- **Mac:** the App Store, later.
- **Windows 10 or 11, and Linux:** coming. Installers will be on the [releases page](https://github.com/jpetkevich/open-holo-releases/releases), each with `SHA256SUMS` so you can check a download (`sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS`).
