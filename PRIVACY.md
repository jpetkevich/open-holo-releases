# Holofoyl Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026. Fordyce Consulting Group, Inc., Rancho Cordova, California. Contact: support@fordycecg.com.

## In short
Holofoyl runs on your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your collection stays on your device unless you turn on iCloud sync or save backups to a synced folder. To get card data, the app downloads public files from Fordyce's card data service. Every copy of the app asks for the same files for the games it has. Like any website, the service sees your IP address and which files your device asks for. Fordyce keeps no logs of these requests and doesn't sell or share anything about you.

## What stays on your device
Your collection, card photos, wishlist, decks, sales, prices and settings are stored on your device. Keys for services you connect are stored in your device's secure keychain.

If you turn on iCloud sync on iPhone or iPad, your collection, locations, tags, wishlist, sales, decks, card photos and game choices are stored in your own iCloud account, under Apple's terms. Fordyce can't see them. Card data and images aren't synced: each device downloads its own.

On computers, Holofoyl saves a daily backup of your collection to a folder on your device. If you choose a folder that a cloud storage service syncs, the backups are stored there, under that service's terms.

## Who your device talks to
- **Our card data service** at catalog.holotcg.io, and its hosting provider. It sends card facts, rules text, small card pictures and prices, built from the card data sources below. While the app is open, your device checks it every hour and downloads each game's new files once a day. The files are the same for everyone. The hosting provider receives your IP address, your app's version and which files are requested.
- **The card data sources** our service is built from, credited on the app's About screen. When you open a card, your device gets its full-size picture and flavour text from the source; Fordyce's service never carries them. If Fordyce turns its service off, the app gets card data, pictures and prices from these sources directly.
- **Our release hosting,** where Fordyce publishes the version file, the sponsor card's picture, and the Windows and Linux downloads.
- **The app store** you installed the app from.
- **AI services you connect** with your own key, and a local AI model if you set one up. Card photos are sent to an AI service only if you choose that recognition mode.

These services receive your IP address and the requests your device makes, under their own privacy policies.

## The version file and remote switches
The app reads Fordyce's version file to learn whether a newer version exists and which features are on. Fordyce can use it to turn off a game, a card data source, a kind of data such as small pictures, or a price source. Turning a price source off deletes that source's prices from your device. They download again if it's turned back on. Your collection, photos and everything else stay.

## Buy links and the sponsor card
Buy buttons open a card's page on TCGplayer's store in your web browser. The link carries Fordyce's affiliate code, so TCGplayer and its affiliate network can tell the visit came from Holofoyl, and Fordyce may earn a commission on a purchase. Holofoyl itself never contacts TCGplayer and does not record or count these clicks. You can hide every Buy button in Settings.

The app may show one sponsor card, the same for everyone. It is not targeted, and the app reports nothing about whether you see or tap it. Tapping it opens the sponsor's website in your browser, under the sponsor's own privacy policy.

## When you email us
Report a problem opens an email to support@fordycecg.com in your own mail app. It is sent only if you send it, and it holds what you see in it: your app version, device and games, and recent errors if you choose to include them.

## Children
Holofoyl has no accounts and doesn't ask anyone, including children, for personal information.

## Changes
We will publish any change to this policy with a new effective date.
